Suppressions API

Manage account-level suppressions and topics (suppression groups) that stop mail to bounced, complained or opted-out recipients.

Suppressions API

Suppressions stop mail to addresses that should not receive it. NorthRelay has two layers:

  • Account-level suppressions block an address for your whole account. NorthRelay adds them automatically on hard bounces, spam complaints and "unsubscribe from all"; you can also add them yourself.
  • Topics (stored as suppression groups) let a recipient opt out of one category of mail, such as "Product updates", while still receiving the rest.

To subscribe or unsubscribe a recipient with the right side effects (contact status, list membership, webhooks), use the Subscriptions API. The routes on this page manage the underlying records directly.

Scopes: suppressions:read for GET, suppressions:write for everything else, on both /suppressions/** and /suppression-groups/**.

How suppressions apply when you send

NorthRelay filters every recipient before the message is queued. Filtered recipients are dropped and do not count against your monthly quota.

MailBlocked by
Marketing (campaigns, marketing message streams)Any account-level suppression; contact status UNSUBSCRIBED, BOUNCED, COMPLAINED or CLEANED; an opt-out of the topic the message is sent under
TransactionalOnly Bounce and Complaint suppressions and contact status BOUNCED, COMPLAINED or CLEANED. Unsubscribes do not block receipts and password resets.

reasonAdded whenLifted by a subscribe call?

Remove Suppression

Only remove a Bounce or Complaint suppression when you have confirmed the address is valid and wants your mail. Sending to addresses that bounced or complained damages your sender reputation and the shared sending IP.

Topics (Suppression Groups)

A topic is a category of mail that recipients can opt out of on its own. Each topic is a suppression group, and a member of the group is an address that opted out of that topic. "Subscribing" someone to a topic means removing them from the group.

Topics take effect on marketing mail when you send:

  • a campaign with a subscription topic set,
  • a campaign to a list that is tagged with the topic (suppressionGroupId on the list),
  • a marketing-stream API message with "suppressionGroupId": "<topicId>" on the send request.

Recipients who opted out of the topic are skipped. Transactional mail ignores topic opt-outs. Recipients can manage published topics themselves on NorthRelay's hosted preference centre ({{preferences_url}}), or on your own page through GET / PATCH /subscriptions/{email}.

Prefer the Subscriptions API for opt-ins and opt-outs: POST /subscriptions/unsubscribe with scope: "topic" also removes the recipient from lists tagged with the topic and fires contact.unsubscribed. The member routes below only edit the group.

Deleting a topic deletes its opt-outs; those addresses become eligible for that mail again.

Topic opt-outs (members)

GET returns { email, createdAt } rows (paginated with page and limit). POST opts an address out (idempotent, returns 201). DELETE removes the opt-out. The older paths /suppression-groups/{id}/suppressions, /suppressions/{email} and /suppressions/bulk remain available.
