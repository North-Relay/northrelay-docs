Mailing Lists and Newsletters

Build newsletter signup, double opt-in, a preference page and unsubscribe handling in your own app on top of NorthRelay.

Mailing Lists and Newsletters

This guide shows how to build mailing-list features into your own product with NorthRelay: a newsletter signup, double opt-in, a preference page inside your app, unsubscribe links, and keeping your database in sync. NorthRelay holds the audience and enforces the opt-out rules; your app calls the API and listens to webhooks.

You need an API key with contacts:read and contacts:write (and suppressions:write if you create topics through the API). Campaigns are available on Micro and above; lists, contacts and the Subscriptions API work on every plan. On Free, subscriptions and signup forms can activate up to 100 contacts.

The building blocks

ConceptWhat it meansAPI
ContactOne recipient address in your account, with status (PENDING, ACTIVE, UNSUBSCRIBED, BOUNCED, COMPLAINED, CLEANED) and consent evidenceContacts
ListAn audience you send campaigns to, such as "Monthly newsletter"Contact lists
TopicA kind of mail people can opt out of on its own, such as "Product updates" or "Events". Stored as a suppression group whose members are the people who opted out.Topics
Global unsubscribe"Stop all marketing mail." Contact becomes UNSUBSCRIBED and an account-level Unsubscribe suppression is added.Subscriptions

Lists or topics? Use a list for "who do I send this to" and a topic for "what kind of mail is this". A common setup is one topic per kind of mail, and each list tagged with its topic. Then:

  • opting out of the topic removes the person from every list tagged with it, and
  • any marketing send under that topic (a campaign to a tagged list, a campaign with that subscription topic, or a marketing-stream API send with suppressionGroupId) skips people who opted out, whichever list they are on.

Campaigns go only to current members of the list: someone who unsubscribed from the list (scope: "list") or has not yet confirmed a double opt-in (PENDING) is not sent the campaign. Tag your lists with topics as well, so that one opt-out covers every list and every send of that kind of mail.

3. Double opt-in

With doubleOptIn: true:

  1. The contact is created as PENDING and added to the lists. Campaigns skip it while it is PENDING.
  2. NorthRelay emails a confirmation link (https://app.northrelay.ca/c/<token>, valid for 7 days). Pass confirmationTemplateId to use your own template; it can use {{confirm_url}}, {{first_name}}, {{last_name}} and {{email}}.
  3. The recipient clicks; the contact becomes ACTIVE, the contact.confirmed webhook fires, and the browser is redirected to your redirectUrl (https:// only).

If someone says the email never arrived, call POST /api/v1/subscriptions/{email}/resend-confirmation. It re-sends the email and gives the link another 7 days.

Double opt-in is the strongest consent record you can have, and it keeps typos and bots off your list, which protects your deliverability. Use it for anything public-facing.

4. Keep consent evidence (CASL and GDPR)

Canada's anti-spam law (CASL) and the EU's GDPR expect you to show when and how each person agreed to receive marketing mail. For every API subscribe call and every signup form submission, NorthRelay stores on the contact:

Let signed-in users manage their email from your own settings screen. Read the current state with GET /subscriptions/{email} and save their choices with PATCH /subscriptions/{email}.

Things to know:

  • GET returns 200 with contact: null for someone who never subscribed, so the page works for every user.
  • topics lists every topic in your account. If some are internal, filter them out.
  • Turning a topic back on does not undo "unsubscribe from all". To bring someone back, the user has to opt in again; then call POST /subscriptions (or PATCH with lists) with "resubscribe": true and fresh consent.

6. Unsubscribe links and one-click unsubscribe

Every marketing message needs a working unsubscribe link, and Gmail, Yahoo and Outlook require RFC 8058 one-click unsubscribe headers for bulk senders. NorthRelay handles both:

  • Campaigns: put {{unsubscribe_url}} in your template footer. NorthRelay adds the List-Unsubscribe and List-Unsubscribe-Post: List-Unsubscribe=One-Click headers to every message.
  • Preference link: put {{preferences_url}} next to it to give recipients a signed link to NorthRelay's hosted preference centre, where they can choose topics or unsubscribe from everything.
  • API mail on a marketing message stream: NorthRelay injects the unsubscribe footer and the same headers automatically. Unsubscribes from these messages are attributed to your account and fire contact.unsubscribed, even when the recipient is not one of your contacts.

When a mailbox provider's "Unsubscribe" button is pressed, NorthRelay unsubscribes the recipient from all marketing mail and fires contact.unsubscribed with method: "one_click". A plain GET of the one-click URL never unsubscribes anyone, because security scanners prefetch links.

Notes:

  • Tokens are signed and expire. INVALID_TOKEN means the link is too old or was altered.
  • The page must not unsubscribe on page load (link scanners open it). Do it when the recipient presses a button.
  • The List-Unsubscribe header still points at NorthRelay, so one-click from the mailbox provider keeps working without your page.
  • For opt-outs made by a signed-in user in your product, skip the token and call POST /api/v1/subscriptions/unsubscribe from your server with your API key.

Before importing:

  • Imports do not fire webhooks. Sync your app from your source data, not from webhooks, for the initial load.

10. Bounce and complaint protection

NorthRelay protects your sender reputation, and the shared sending IP, automatically:

  • A hard bounce adds a Bounce suppression; a spam complaint adds a Complaint suppression. Neither is ever lifted by a subscribe call, and both block even transactional mail.
  • Marketing sends also skip anyone who unsubscribed from everything or opted out of the topic the mail is sent under, and campaigns skip members who left the list or have not confirmed their double opt-in.
  • Adding an existing contact to a list does not make it mailable again: suppressed and unsubscribed members stay in the list but are skipped at send time and shown with suppressionStatuses in GET /contacts/lists/{id}/members.
  • Suppressed recipients are dropped before quota is counted.

Keep complaint rates low by using double opt-in, sending what people signed up for, putting the preference and unsubscribe links where they are easy to find, and removing people who have not engaged in a long time.

Checklist

  • Topics created, with public labels, and each list tagged with its topic
  • Signup calls POST /subscriptions from your server with doubleOptIn, redirectUrl and consent
  • Signup handler handles RECIPIENT_UNSUBSCRIBED and RECIPIENT_SUPPRESSED
  • Campaign templates include {{unsubscribe_url}} and {{preferences_url}}
  • Preference page uses GET and PATCH /subscriptions/{email}
  • Webhook endpoint verifies signatures and handles contact.* events
  • Old provider's suppressions imported before the contacts
